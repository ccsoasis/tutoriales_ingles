# Explanation of the conditions that make the blades turn

!!! tip "🎯 Why the blades turn"
    ***Objective:*** Relate the wind energy with the aerodynamic torque generation and the start of the rotor turning.
    ***What to observe:*** Wind, rotor speed, torque (if available) and turbine state during the start of movement.
    ***What to note:*** Wind conditions from which the rotor begins to turn and how the speed changes when the wind increases.
    💡 compare the behavior with stable wind vs slow variations to see the rotor response.

In this practice the idea is to observe the "**Total wind**" concept incident on the blade edge, both in intensity and in direction relative to the blade pitch angle.

The objective is to observe how the control of the power obtained from the wind is produced, as explained in [Why do the blades turn?](../DSASTutorial/PFAPorquegiranlaspalas.md), and see how small pitch variations produce the blade stall entry, and, therefore, the following are produced:

- If in Production: the power obtained from the wind variation

- If in a braking operation, how this power is *delivered to the wind*.
