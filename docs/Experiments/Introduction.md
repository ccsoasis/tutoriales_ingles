# Introduction to Wind Turbine Experiments

### 🌬️ Welcome to the Wind Turbine Virtual Laboratory

This manual will guide you through a series of practical experiments designed to understand the operation of different wind turbine technologies through virtual simulation.

### What will you find in this manual?

This document contains the detailed description of practical experiments that you can carry out using the Wind Farm Simulator simulator suite. These simulators work as virtual work benches that allow you to experiment with different wind turbine types without the need for physical equipment.

!!! info "💻 About Wind Farm Simulator"
    **Wind Farm Simulator** is an educational simulator suite that reproduces the behavior of different wind turbine families under realistic conditions. It allows you to:

    - Observe the system behavior in real time
    - Modify operational and configuration parameters
    - Carry out measurements and comparisons
    - Experiment with different wind conditions
    - Analyze the performance of different technologies

### Manual structure

Each experiment in this manual includes:

#### 📋 Components of each experiment:

- **Learning objectives:** What you will learn when completing the experiment
- **Theoretical foundations:** Concepts necessary to understand the experiment
- **Step-by-step procedure:** Detailed instructions to carry out the experiment
- **Associated tutorials:** Links to complementary in-depth material
- **Analysis activities:** Tasks to consolidate the learning

### Learning methodology

This manual is designed following an active learning methodology. Do not limit yourself to reading: the objective is that you **experiment, observe, analyze and compare** the results obtained.

!!! info "🎯 Pedagogical approach"
    The experiments are organized so that you develop a progressive understanding:

    1. **Understanding of basic principles:** How and why wind turbines work

    2. **Operation and control:** Orientation, pitch and regulation systems

    3. **Dynamic response:** Behavior against wind condition changes

    4. **Economic aspects:** Operating costs and energy efficiency

### How to use this manual

#### 📖 Recommendations to make the most of it:

- **Follow the proposed order:** The experiments are didactically sequenced

- **Read the tutorials:** Before each experiment, review the indicated tutorials

- **Take notes:** Record your observations and results

- **Experiment freely:** Once an experiment is completed, modify parameters and observe the effects

- **Compare technologies:** Many experiments will ask you to compare different wind turbine types

### Wind turbine families you will study

Throughout the experiments you will work with three main wind turbine families:

!!! info "⚡ Wind turbine technologies:"
    1. **DSAS (Direct Stall Active Stall):** Squirrel-cage generator directly connected to the grid, with active aerodynamic stall control
    2. **RRC (Rotor Resistance Control):** Wound rotor generator with rotor resistance control
    3. **DFIG (Doubly Fed Induction Generator):** Doubly fed induction generator with back-to-back converters

### Prerequisites

To make the most of these experiments, it is recommended that you have basic knowledge of:

- Physics (mechanics, electricity)

- Rotating electrical machines

- Basic wind energy concepts
