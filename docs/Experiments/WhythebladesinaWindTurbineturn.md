# Why do the blades of a wind turbine turn?

!!! tip "🎯 Learning objective"
    At the end of this section, you will understand the fundamental physical principle that allows the conversion of the wind kinetic energy into rotational mechanical energy in a wind turbine.

### Introduction: The role of the blades in the wind turbine

The blades are the key elements where the energy conversion occurs in a wind turbine. Specifically, the blades transform part of the wind kinetic energy into rotational kinetic energy of the wind turbine power train (drivetrain).

### How is this conversion produced?

This energy conversion occurs thanks to the aerodynamic forces that are generated when the wind passes through the blades. These forces cause the rotor rotational movement.

!!! info "💭 Common misconception"
    At first sight, it might seem that the wind forces simply "push" the blades perpendicularly to the rotation plane, as if the wind hit against a vertical wall that turns.
    **However, this explanation is incorrect.** The real operating principle is much more sophisticated and efficient.

### The real aerodynamic principle: Lift

The true reason why the blades turn **is not intuitive**, but it is fundamental to understand it to understand the operation of a wind turbine.

!!! warning "⚡ Key principle"
    The wind turbine blades work following a principle similar to the one that allows **airplanes to stay in the air during flight**.
    This principle is based on the generation of lift forces produced by the blade aerodynamic profile.

### Summary of key concepts

!!! info "📝 Important points to remember:"
    1. The blades convert wind kinetic energy into rotational energy
    2. This conversion is produced through aerodynamic forces
    3. The principle is NOT simply wind "push"
    4. The real principle is similar to airplane lift
    5. The blade aerodynamic profile is essential for this operation
