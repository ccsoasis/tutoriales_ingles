# Active copies of the Recorder

The "***Active copy***" concept allows having "photos" of the signal evolution graph of a Recorder at a certain instant and, additionally, allows making time measurements on them.

### Indication of the moment associated with the cursor

When the mouse is over the synoptic, the moment corresponding to that position is indicated in "Current."

![](<lib/Hardcopy en espanol.jpg>)

Pressing the right mouse button when it is over this synoptic allows fixing the instant that will serve as origin of time measurements. This action is marked with a yellow line. From that moment, when moving the mouse over the synoptic, a vertical blue line is presented, that follows it, while the time distance is indicated in the indicator titled "*Delta time*".

### Measuring the time distance between two events

When the "Time reference" has been set, a yellow vertical line appears in all the "Recorders," and the upper area shows two time values: "Ref," which indicates the reference moment, and "Delta time." The latter indicator shows the time distance (in milliseconds) between the "Time reference" and the current moment where the mouse cursor is located (blue line).

![](<lib/hardcopy con medida de tiempo espanol.jpg>)

The ![](<lib/Boton copia al clipboard.jpg>) button allows making a copy that passes to the clipboard, so that it can be included in any Office application.
