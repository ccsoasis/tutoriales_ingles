# Electrical scheme

The "**Electrical scheme**" tab of the "[Electrical Panel](Panelelctrico.md)" shows a simplified scheme.

![](<lib/Panel electrico - esquema.jpg>) Fig 1

In the scheme the following are represented:

- The power cable connection, from the grid, in the lower part,

- ○fuses,

- ○the "*Main switch*" contactor, the bars where it is connected:

- ■the *Thyristor* groups and their "*Bypass*" contactors,

- ■the current measuring transformers.

- ○cables that go up to the nacelle.

- The capacitor bank intended to correct the power factor.

- The controller (P.L.C.) with the grid signal measurement interfaces (Current meas.) and control of the Thyristor gates, their Bypasses and the capacitor bank.

The grid connection detail scheme is the same described in the Two-Speed and Active Stall simulator tutorial.
