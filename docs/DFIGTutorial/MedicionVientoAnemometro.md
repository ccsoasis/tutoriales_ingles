# Wind measurement - description of an anemometer.

The anemometer is the device that measures the wind in the nacelle. It is normally placed at the rear of the nacelle, so in reality, it measures the wind not taken by the blades.

The simplest anemometer model is shown in the following video. It is normally a device that generates a digital signal whose frequency depends on the wind speed. This is a device that must be calibrated.

![](<lib/Anemometro.gif>)

A typical curve for this type of anemometer is shown in the following figure. They are usually quite linear, with one or two slope change points.

![](<lib/Anemometer curve.jpg>)

There are other types of anemometers, such as those based on a hot wire (see technical details here or here), as well as those based on sonar-like techniques (see details [here](http://www.minisodar.de/products_mini_sodar.htm)).
