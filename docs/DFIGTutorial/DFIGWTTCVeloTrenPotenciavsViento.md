# Power Train speed vs Wind

## Diagram of the Power Train (Rotor) speed as a function of the Wind

This panel presents significant aspects of the evolution of the Power Train revolutions when the Controller implements the control mechanism implicit in the Operational Curve of Variable Speed Wind Turbines, as described in [Implications of the desired behavior](Implicacionesdelcomportamientode.md).

![](<lib/DFIG RotorvsViento.jpg>)

This graph shows 4 well-differentiated zones:

1. First, when the wind is in the range where the Operational Curve coincides with the MPPT (Maximum Power Point Tracking) curve, the rpms are on a straight line (zone between points B and C).

2. Second, when the wind exceeds the Nominal Wind, the Power Train rotation speed in steady state (when it has stabilized) is fixed.

3. The other two zones are transition zones to those described in points 1 and 2 above.

It is also a good diagram to follow the dynamic evolution of the wind turbine during wind gusts.
