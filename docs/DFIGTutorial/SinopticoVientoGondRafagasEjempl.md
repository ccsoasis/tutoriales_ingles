# Examples

In this section, three examples of wind flow concentration are presented to show how they can be used to study the evolution of the wind turbine signals.

Se ha utilizado el StripRecorder "*Cutin + Coutout + Production: Panel to monitor cutin and cutout operations*".

The three series use different values for the variable "Nacelle.BladeSlewRate" (i.e. different Pitch angle controllers), the flash file and the initial wind.

## First series:

BladeSlewRate= (5 º/s)/s ; Gust="StandardGust120x4" ; initial wind = 7 followed by 8, 9 and 10 m/s.

## Second series:

BladeSlewRate= (5 º/s)/s ; Gust="StandardGust120x4" ; initial wind = 11 followed by 12, 13, 15 and 16 m/s.
