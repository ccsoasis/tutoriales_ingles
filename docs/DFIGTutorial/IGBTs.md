# IGBT

**IGBTs**

#### (Insulated gated bipolar transistor)

It's a bipolar transistor controlled by an isolated door.

The switch between the induction and the common is implemented, nowadays, with an electronic device name IGBT (Isolated Gate Bipolar Transistor). These devices are able to sustain high voltages when in the open mode (in the order of 1200V to 3500V) while conducting high currents in the closed mode (thousands of Amps). They are the most stressed components in the convert and therefore that most influence its reliability. This characteristic also conditions how the are delivered, normally in packages together with protection diodes or set of several units to ease the coupling with heat sinks. (see below
