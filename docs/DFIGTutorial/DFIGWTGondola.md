# Views of the nacelle

## Power Train synoptic

![](<lib/SinopticDriveTrain -3D-Instrumentacion - espa.jpg>)

This synoptic allows:

1. Presenting a 3D view of the wind turbine placing it inside a Wind Farm. It is accessed through the "Views" menu.

2. Presenting an internal view of the nacelle components. See [Internal view of the nacelle](DFIGWTGondolaVistaInterna.md).

3. Access to modify the temperature of key elements. See [Internal view of the nacelle](DFIGWTGondolaVistaInterna.md).

4. Access to the visualization of internal variables and modification (of some).

5. Access to the visualization and control of a number of important internal variables: "Instrumentation View".
