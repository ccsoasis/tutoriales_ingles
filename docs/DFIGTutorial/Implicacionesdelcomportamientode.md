# Implications of the desired behavior

In this chapter the implications derived from the behavior described in the previous chapters are developed.

We recall again that a wind turbine can be seen as two energy converters that work together: one to convert the wind kinetic energy into rotational kinetic energy, made up of the Hub, the Blades and the Rotor, known as "*Power Train*" (*DriveTrain* in English), and another that converts the rotational energy of this Power Train into electrical energy: the electric Generator.
