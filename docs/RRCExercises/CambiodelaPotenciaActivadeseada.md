# Desired Active Power change

!!! tip "🎯 Desired Active Power change"
    ***Objective:*** Explore the RRC wind turbine capacity to limit the active power delivered to the grid to a value below the nominal, and observe how the operating point changes in the "Power vs Slip" synoptic.
    ***What to observe:*** The operating point displacement in "Power vs Slip" when reducing the desired active power from 660 kW to 400 kW with wind above the nominal, and the associated Pitch and DutyCycle changes.
    💡 When reducing the desired power, does the Pitch, the DutyCycle or both change? Which is the main limitation mechanism?

!!! warning "❓ Guiding questions"
    1. When changing the "Desired Active Power" from 660 kW to 400 kW with 14 m/s, to which new point of the "Power vs Slip" curve does the operating point move?
    2. Does the Pitch change when reducing the desired active power? And the DutyCycle? Which is the mechanism that adjusts the power?
    3. What happens to the power dissipated in the rotor circuit (RCCPower) when the active power is limited? Does it increase or decrease?

In this practice the objective is to observe the Desired Active Power control influence on the different system variables.

Put the wind turbine in Production, with a wind above the nominal, for example, 14 m/s.

In the "Desired Active Power" indicator of the "Control Terminal" change the value from 660 kW to 400 kW and observe the "Power vs Slip" synoptic and you will see that the new operating point has changed, as shown in the following figure.
