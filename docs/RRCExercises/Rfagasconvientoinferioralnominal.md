# Gusts with wind below the nominal

!!! tip "🎯 Gusts with wind below the nominal"
    ***Section objective:*** Explore the RRC wind turbine behavior against wind gusts when the starting wind is below the nominal (~12 m/s), observing whether the rotor resistance control acts or the system behaves like a squirrel-cage generator.
    ***What to observe:*** The DutyCycle (PWM) value, the operating point position in the "Power vs Slip" synoptic and whether the power follows the wind shape.
    💡 With low wind, the PWM remains at 100%. Does that mean the RRC control is active or inactive?
