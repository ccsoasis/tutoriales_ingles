# rpms control mechanism observation

!!! tip "🎯 RPMs control mechanism observation"
    ***Objective:*** Consolidate the learning of all the previous practices observing the working point variation in the "Power vs Slip" synoptic and checking the RRC mechanism operating rules.
    ***What to observe:*** The operating point position (yellow) on the A-D-C-E curve sections, its relationship with the red line (minimum resistance), and the power dissipated in the rotor circuit ("RCCPower" indicator).
    💡 The power dissipated in the rotor resistance (RCCPower) is proportional to the slip. Check it by measuring in different conditions.

!!! warning "❓ Guiding questions"
    1. When the wind is below the nominal, on which line of the "Power vs Slip" synoptic is the operating point located? Why?
    2. When the wind exceeds the nominal, is the operating point always point B in steady state? What happens during the transitions (gusts)?
    3. Is the statement that the power dissipated in the rotor (RCCPower) is proportional to the slip true? Verify this relationship with at least two different wind values above the nominal.

In this practice the objective is to repeat all the previous classes but observing the working point variation on the "*Power vs Slip*" synoptic.

The points to verify are the following:

- While the wind is below the nominal (around 12 m/s) the operating point is on the red line that indicates the minimum rotor resistance, the one corresponding to squirrel-cage operation.  
![](<lib/Pot vs deslizacion con 6 ms.jpg>)

- When the wind is above the nominal, the working point is always point B, except in the adaptation phases.  
![](<lib/Punto trabajo viento superior al nominal.jpg>)

- When gusts are applied observe that the working point is always on the A-D-C-E sections, indicated in the following figure.  
![](<lib/Pot vs Desliz con puntos indicados.jpg>)

Another very important point to observe is the power dissipated in the rotor circuit variation, indicated at all times in the "Control Terminal" synoptic in the "*RCCPower*" indicator.

![](<lib/RCCPower.jpg>)

You can check that this dissipated power is proportional to the slip.
