# Stop with wind above the Nominal

!!! tip "🎯 Stop with wind above the Nominal"
    ***Objective:*** Compare the sequence and duration of the ordered stop with wind above the nominal (~13 m/s) with respect to the stop with low wind, and understand why it is faster.
    ***What to observe:*** The Pitch evolution, the mechanical and electrical powers, and the total stop time compared to the low wind case.
    💡 Why is the stop faster with high wind, despite having greater initial kinetic energy?

!!! warning "❓ Guiding questions"
    1. How long does the stop sequence last with 13 m/s wind? Compare it with the ~17 seconds of the 7 m/s case.
    2. Why is the stop faster with high wind, if there is more kinetic energy in the Power Train?
    3. Is the event sequence (Pitch → negative mechanical power → B2B stop → total stop) the same as with low wind? Is there any difference?

Repeat the experiment in [Stop with wind below the Nominal](CPParoVientoMenorNominal.md) but with a wind above the Nominal, for example 13 m/s. You will obtain a signal evolution similar to the one shown in the following graph.

## Explanation

The signal evolution in this case is similar to the case of wind below the nominal, with the difference that the duration of the stop sequence is shorter in the case of high winds.

That is, with 7 m/s wind this duration is in the order of 17 seconds (see [Stop with wind below the Nominal](CPParoVientoMenorNominal.md)) while in this case (13 m/s wind) it is 12 seconds. That is, when the Pitch angle makes the Cp negative, the more wind the greater the negative torque produced by the blades, that is, the sooner it stops, despite the difference in initial kinetic energy values.
