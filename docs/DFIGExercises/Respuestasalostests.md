# Answers to the tests

*Test 1:*  
*In steady state (that is, when enough time has passed since the last wind change) the blade rotation speed, when the wind is above the nominal:*  
  
*a) Is fixed (does not depend on the wind value).*

*Test 2:*  
*In steady state the blade rotation speed, when the wind is below the nominal:*  
  
*Answer:*  
  
*b) Is variable (depends on the wind value).*

*Test 3:*  
*When the Desired Active Power changes and the wind remains constant: does the blade angle (Pitch) vary?*  
  
*Answer:*  
  
*c) It depends on the wind value and the Desired Active Power.*  
  
*If the Desired Active Power is lower than the current wind Power, the Pitch must increase, to reduce the Cp and therefore the mechanical power obtained from the wind.*

*Test 4:*  
*When the Desired Reactive Power changes and the wind remains constant: does the blade angle (Pitch) vary?*  
  
*Answer:*  
  
*a) No.*

*Test 5:*  
*When the wind turbine is in production and the following wind sequence occurs:*  
*-First it drops below the minimum Wind for Production.*  
*-Then it rises above the Minimum Wind for Cutin:*  
  
*Which of these answers is true?*  
  
*Answer:*  
  
*b) It stops and starts up when the wind is sufficient, without waiting for the maintenance technician to give the "START" order.*

*Test 6:*  
*When the DFIG wind turbine is in production and a momentary voltage drop occurs in one phase of the grid and a rapid recovery: which of these answers is true?*  
  
*Answer:*  
  
*a) It stops and remains waiting for the maintenance technician to give the "START" order.*
