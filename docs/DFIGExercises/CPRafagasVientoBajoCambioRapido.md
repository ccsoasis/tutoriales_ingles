# With rapid changes

!!! tip "🎯 Low wind gusts with rapid changes"
    ***Objective:*** Compare the wind turbine behavior against rapid gusts ("Standard60x10", amplitude 10 m/s) with the slow gust case, observing when the Pitch comes into action and the Power Train kinetic storage effect.
    ***What to observe:*** The Pitch activation when the wind exceeds the nominal (~11 m/s), the kinetic energy "storage" effect during the gust, and the widening of the Electrical Power with respect to the wind pulse.
    💡 The electrical power is "wider" than the wind gust. What physical phenomenon explains this?

!!! warning "❓ Guiding questions"
    1. From which wind speed does the Pitch begin to change during the "Standard60x10" gust? Does it coincide with the nominal wind?
    2. Why does the Electrical Power delivered to the grid continue increasing even after the wind has already begun to descend?
    3. Compare the Electrical Power response between the "Standard120x4" and "Standard60x10" gust. What is the main difference? What is it due to?

Repeat the experiment described in [With slow changes](CPRafagasVientoBajoCambioLento.md), but using the "Standard60x10" wind gust. You will obtain a graph similar to the following.

![](<lib/WindGust 60x10 sobre 7 m-s.jpg>)

In this case, contrary to what is seen with less pronounced gusts, there is a Pitch change in the gust interval. You can check that the Pitch begins to change when the wind passes the Nominal Wind value (around 11 m/s).

The result, compared to the case in which a less pronounced gust is used ("Standard120x4"), is that the evolution of the Electrical Power delivered to the grid is wider than the wind evolution interval. This occurs because a storage effect of the wind mechanical energy occurs when the wind exceeds the Nominal Wind, in the form of ***additional*** kinetic energy of the Power Train. This extra energy is transferred to the grid later, after the wind gust has disappeared.
