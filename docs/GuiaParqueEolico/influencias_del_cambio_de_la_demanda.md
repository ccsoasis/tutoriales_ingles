# Demand Change Influences

The production of a Wind Farm must follow the planning determined by the wind that affects it, and must take into account the market demands, as well as the operations required by the Electrical Grid (TSO).

It may be that, in certain circumstances (for example the evacuation line disconnection due to an accident, a phase-to-ground short circuit, etc.), it is necessary to stop all the wind turbines.

You can simulate this circumstance **reducing the demanded power to 0**. If you have the "**Automatic Control**" selected in the SCADA grid, you can observe the decisions the SCADA makes.

Follow the "*Practical Class*" "**[Demand Change](../WFSSCADA/Influenciadelascondicionesambien.md)**" instructions in the tutorial.
