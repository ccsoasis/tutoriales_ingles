# Control Center Simulator of a Wind Farm

This document has the following objectives:

- Describe the **Simulator structure**, indicating the correspondences of its elements with those found in the reality of a Wind Farm.

- Describe the **functionalities of the different Farm elements**, and their corresponding simulations in the Simulator.

- Explain a **set of Exercises to be carried out by the students**, in order to become familiar with the work carried out in a Wind Farm.

- Launch the **Wind Farm Simulator execution**
