# Main synoptic

The main synoptic of this simulator has the following parts:

- Schematic of the **wind turbine connectivity with the Substation with indication of each wind turbine state and its current generated power.** (Upper part).

- The **meteorological station** simulator. (Located in the lower left part).

- The **production Demand** simulator. (Located in the lower right part).

- The **wind turbine state and control** visualization interface simulator. (In the central part).

- Indication of **total power that is being produced in the farm**.

- The interface for **wind turbine state and efficiency change**.

- The access to the **3D visualization**.

- The operation control of the **automatic control algorithms** of wind turbine start/stop.
