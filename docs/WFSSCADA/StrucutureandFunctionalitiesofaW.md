# Structure and Functionalities of a Wind Farm

The elements of a Wind Farm are:

- The **Wind Turbines**.

- The **electrical Substation** with which it connects to the Electrical Grid.

- The **set of power cables** that connect each one of the Wind Turbines with the Substation.

- The **electrical signal measurement system**, located in the Substation.

- The **meteorological condition measurement instruments**: wind speed and direction, ambient temperature, humidity and atmospheric pressure.

- The Farm Supervision and Control System (**SCADA** by its acronym in English **S**upervisory **C**ontrol **A**nd **D**ata **A**cquisition).

- **Reactive Power control and management elements** (capacitor banks, STATCOMs, etc.)

- The **communications systems between the SCADA and the Wind Turbines**.

- The **Farm communications systems with other Supervision and Control centers, outside the Farm**.

The functionalities available within a Wind Farm have to do with the following topics:

- **Operational control of the Wind Turbines**

- **Management of the Maintenance operations:** planned, preventive, corrective.

- Recording of all the operations carried out within the Farm.

- All the management operations necessary for the compliance with the **electrical production supply contracts** that have been agreed with the clients.

Generally, the majority of these functionalities are carried out through the SCADA.
