# Control of a Wind Turbine

In [What is a Wind Turbine?](WhatisaWindTurbine.md) the industrial Wind Turbine components are described. From the *Wind Farm management* point of view, the generic characteristics of an industrial Wind Turbine are the following:

- 1.A Wind Turbine is an *Industrial electrical energy production Plant*, that does not need any exterior information for its operation. That is, they are **autonomous plants**.

- 2.The electrical power it delivers depends on the incident wind speed, but not on its direction (since they orient automatically.) It also depends on control parameters: at a certain moment there may be limits for the power generated by a wind turbine.

- 3.Its power cables, through which it communicates in the last term with the Grid, are connected with the Common Connection Point (PCC), located in the electrical Substation.

- 4.They have communications channels, generally through fiber optics, through which they are connected with the farm [SCADA](TheControlRoom.md). Through these means:

- 1.Its operation can be controlled.  
This control includes the automatic start ("START") and the ordered stop ("STOP"). According to the wind turbine type, it is also possible to remotely control the *maximum active power* and the *reactive power* to deliver to the grid.

- 2.The wind turbine responds with information relative to its state.  
The delivered information includes, typically:

- 1.Its *state*: in production or stopped.

- 2.The *grid voltage levels*.

- 3.The *power* it is generating, active and reactive.

- 4.*Wind*: speed and direction.

- 5.The *produced energy* from a preset instant.

- 6.*Temperatures* of key elements: ambient, generator, gearbox, bearings, lubricating oils, etc.

- 7.Certain operating parameters with which it is working: power limits, etc.

- 8.*Incidents* (alarms, messages, etc.) generated by the Controller.
