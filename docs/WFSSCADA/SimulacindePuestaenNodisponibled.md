# Visualization of the temporal evolution of the signals in the SCADA

Read the indications in [Signal evolution graphs.](PowerpergroupTotalproducedPowera.md)

Repeat the previous practical classes while having a "***Recorder***" open that presents the "***Wind, Total power, Power per Group***" panel.

You will obtain a representation like the one in the following figure.

![](<lib/Registrador SCADA.png>)

The first recorder indicates the wind speed evolution.

The second presents three powers:

- The Demand, in blue.

- The Total power in white.

- The maximum available power, in green.

- In each one of the following recorders the production of each one of the wind turbines per "Group" (A, F, E) is presented. It can be appreciated that all the wind turbines produce a similar power, although not the same. It can also be appreciated when the wind turbines enter and leave production.
