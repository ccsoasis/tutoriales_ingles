# Wind turbine connections to the PCC

The upper part of the main synoptic presents a graph with the shape of the wind turbine connections to the PCC (electrical Substation).

![](<lib/Sinoptico SCADA - conectividad.png>)

The wind turbine state is also indicated:

| ![](<lib/Sinoptico SCADA - aerogenerador en produccion.png>) | The <mark>green color</mark> of the wind turbine indicates it is in production. The height of the green column to its left indicates the current production, in relation to the nominal. |
|---|---|
| ![](<lib/Sinoptico SCADA aerogenerador parado.png>) | The <mark>yellow color</mark> indicates it is not in production, although it is available, without problems. |
| ![](<lib/Sinoptico SCADA aerogenerador no disponible.png>) | The <mark>red color</mark> indicates the wind turbine is not available: that is, it presents some problem that prevents it from being put in production, or that advises not to put it in production. |
