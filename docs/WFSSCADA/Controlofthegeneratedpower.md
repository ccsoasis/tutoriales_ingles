# About the generated power control

The total power generated by the Farm must always be kept within the ***external setpoint*** marked by the TSO (transport system operator.) This setpoint is a value that *enters* the SCADA. Generally, the wind-origin energy production has priority over other sources, but it is not always so. In [Delivered Active Power control](IntegracionconlaRedElectrica.md) different protocols to carry out said tracking are described.

The *production control* depends on the farm wind turbine type. If these are fixed speed, and/or of those that use the aerodynamic stall entry control method (*Active Stall*), the only control method consists of starting or stopping each one of the wind turbines. In this case the *control granularity* corresponds to the power of one of these wind turbines.

In more modern wind turbines, such as the doubly fed types (DFIG), or with rotor resistance control, or with full power converter (*full converter*), it is possible to control the maximum power produced by each wind turbine, independently. In these cases the control granularity is much finer.

The control of the production of both *active power* and *reactive* is a requirement to be able to consider the wind-origin energy as one more within the energy mix (see [Integration of a Wind Farm in the Electrical Grid.](IntegrationofaWindFarmintheGrid.md))
