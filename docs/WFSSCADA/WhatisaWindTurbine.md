# What is a Wind Turbine?

A wind turbine is an electrical energy production plant from the wind kinetic energy. It can be described with a set formed by a **converter of the wind kinetic energy to rotational kinetic energy**, which is coupled to a **converter of rotational kinetic energy to electrical energy**, that is an electrical generator.

There are several wind turbine types, with a first distinction relative to the **blade arrangement**: vertical axis, or horizontal axis. Practically all the wind turbines in Wind Farms are horizontal axis.

They can also be distinguished according to the **electrical generator type** and **how it is connected to the Electrical Grid**. It also differs in the method of controlling the power obtained from the wind: whether it is through the blade attack angle control (variable or not).

The most common industrial wind turbine type, of those that can be found in wind farms, is the one with blades rigidly joined and turning in a vertical plane that is kept facing the wind; therefore the turning axis is kept. They are known as **HAWT** (**H**orizontal **A**xis **W**ind **T**urbines).

The converter of the wind energy to rotational energy is composed of the blades, which are attached to a "hub", which is in turn coupled to a shaft that transmits the mechanical power to a gearbox. The gearbox output is connected to the electrical generator.

The set formed by the blades, the hub, and the rotor that connects it with the gearbox is known as "**Power Train**" (***DriveTrain*** in English). The Power Train, the *gearbox* (in English), the *brake system*, the *orientation mechanism* (***yaw*** in English) are mounted on a platform that can turn horizontally on the Tower top, and which is known as **Nacelle** (***Nacelle*** in English). The electrical generation system (*electrical generator* and *power electronics*, if applicable) are also located inside the Nacelle. The power cables fall vertically through the Tower center until reaching the electrical cabinet located at its base, where they connect with the output cables to the grid.
