# Territorial deployment of a Wind Farm

The following figure shows the distribution of the different Wind Farm components, where its extension can be seen. The communication roads are in red, the wind turbines are labeled in black, and in blue the trenches where the power and communications cables are found. The level curves are drawn in dark brown. It can be seen that the wind turbines are located in the upper part of a hill, for better wind use.
