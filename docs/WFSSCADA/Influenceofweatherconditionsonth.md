# Environmental conditions influence on the Farm Production

The production of a wind turbine is proportional to the **air density**.

- 1.In the "**Meteorological station**" increase the **ambient temperature**. Check that the air density decreases when the temperature increases, and vice versa.  
![](<lib/Sinoptico SCADA estacion meteorologica.png>)

- 2.Increase the **ambient humidity**. Check that the air density decreases when the humidity increases, and vice versa.

- 3.Increase the **atmospheric pressure**. Check that the air density increases when the pressure increases, and vice versa.

Observe how the Total Production changes correspondingly to the air density changes.

If the "[Automatic control](AutomaticDispatching.md)" is active, and the wind level is adequate, you will be able to see how these changes influence the farm management.
