# Simplified hydraulic scheme

The following figure shows the **simplified hydraulic circuit** for the blade position control.

![](<lib/Cilindro%20hidraulico%20bloqueado.svg>)

Fig 1

The hydraulic cylinder piston is connected to the device that rotates the blade (See [With hydraulic cylinders](Mecanismodecambiodeangulodelasp1.md)).

In the shown position, in which the three-position valve is located in the center, the cylinder hydraulic ducts are connected to nothing, closed (that is, they maintain the pressure corresponding to both piston sides).

When the valve is moved to the right, as in Fig 2, and the pump starts, the piston moves to the right.

![](<lib/Cilindro%20hidraulico%20a%20derechas.svg>)

Fig 2

If, on the contrary, the valve is placed in the left position, as in Fig 3, and the pump starts, the piston will move to the left.

![](<lib/Cilindro%20hidraulico%20a%20izquierdas.svg>)

Fig 3
