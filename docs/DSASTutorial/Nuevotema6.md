## Design concepts of a planetary stage of a gearbox.

## Herringbone gears

![](<lib/herringbone-gears_limpio.png>)

This type of gear has the following characteristics:

- *It does not present any lateral thrust on its support*, in either of the two rotation directions. Normal helical gears make thrusts appear on the rotation shaft where they are placed, due to the thrust on each tooth. The direction of this thrust force depends on the gear rotation direction. This is due to the fact that each of the two parts of each tooth pushes in the opposite direction to the other one. This eliminates the need for the *bearings to support any lateral stress*.

- The power transmission between gears is smoother than in the case of helical ones, due to the fact that there is more contact surface between the teeth, and they "enter" (mesh) more smoothly.

- They have a greater load capacity.

- However they are more difficult to manufacture, due to the additional V-shape complexity.
