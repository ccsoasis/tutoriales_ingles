# Indicator connections

![](<lib/Esquema conexiones indicadores yawing.png>)

The "Left Arrow" and "Right Arrow" indicators are normally in* display-only* mode. In this mode, they show the attached signal value. "Left Arrow" ON indicates counter-clockwise nacelle rotation. "Right Arrow" ON indicates clockwise nacelle rotation.

However, you can switch to *command* mode (F6); in this mode, the indicator becomes fully operational, and the operator can change its value, meaning the indicated value no longer reflects the associated signal.  In this mode, the operator can change the activity of the associated yawing motors at will.

This feature allows for various functions, such as changing the initial orientation or simulating malfunctions.
