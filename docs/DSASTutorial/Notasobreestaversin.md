# What can you learn with this simulator?

With this simulator you will be able to:

- know the **fundamentals and the basic structure of a wind turbine**.

- know the **description of its components, their physical principles, and their behaviors**.

- **observe the evolutions of the internal variables** of each of the subsystems and specific elements, when it enters and exits Production.

- observe the **reaction of its internal variables against changes in its operating conditions**, both normal, and in fault or exceptional ones.

This simulator is based on a type of wind turbine very widespread until the first years of the 2000s decade. It uses a *short-circuited rotor induction electric generator*, also known as squirrel cage, and *can change the blade attack angle* (pitch).

This tutorial is an integral part of the simulator and is organized in the following sections:

- [What is a wind turbine?](Queesunaerogenerador.md)

In this section you will find an introduction to the components of a typical wind turbine, along with the specificities of the family called "Active Stall".

- [Physical principles](PrincipiosFisicosAerogeneradores.md)

Here you will find a description of the fundamentals of the subsystems in a generic wind turbine and specific data related to industrial wind turbines.

- [Active Stall and Two-Speed Wind Turbine Simulator](SimDSAS.md):

This section describes the structure and components of this simulator. Since it runs on the LdP platform, it can also be used as a Test Bench, where the parameters of the different subsystems can be changed to observe several behaviors. You will need to know the concepts about the LdP platform, collected in the "*Introduction to LdP*" tutorial.

- [Practical classes](../DSASExercises/ClasesPracticas.md)

This document contains the guidelines to carry out a set of experiments with which you will be able to reproduce situations that occur in real wind turbines, both in normal and exceptional conditions, and observe their effect on the different internal variables, as well as study the influence of different parameters.

Each class contains the description of the steps to take with the simulator for its study.
