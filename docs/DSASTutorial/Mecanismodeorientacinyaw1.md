# Flexible couplings of the high shaft

Functions of the flexible coupling between the Gearbox and the Generator shaft

The gearbox output shaft is connected to the Generator rotor shaft through a flexible coupling, whose main functions are:

- To avoid the effects of the possible **misalignments** between these two shafts.

- **To limit the mechanical torque transmitted to the Generator**.
