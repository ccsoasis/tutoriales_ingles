# Exterior view

This synoptic presents the *exterior view* of the electrical cabinet, as presented in the following figure.

![](<lib/Armario electrico frontal.png>)

In it the following can be seen:

- The "***Main Switch***". This graphic indicator is active, so that by clicking it with the left mouse button its state changes (connected / disconnected). The state is indicated in the immediately superior indicator.

- The *checkbox* to switch to the "***Internal view***".

- The ***state of the thyristor Bypass contactors***.

- The indication of how the ***current Generator grid connection*** is. It is indicated in "***High speed***", and "***Low speed***", which correspond to the two possible modes: with 4 poles and with 6 poles, respectively.

- Three *checkboxes* for ***control of the three grid phases***, which are located below the Bypass indicator.
