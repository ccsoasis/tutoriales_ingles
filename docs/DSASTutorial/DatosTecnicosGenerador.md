# Characteristics of the Generator emulated in this Simulator

## Generator characteristics

The generator used in the wind turbine on which this simulator is based has two ways of connecting the stator winding, so that it offers the possibility of creating 2 pole pairs (with which the synchronous speed, for 50 Hz, is 1500 rpms) or 3 pole pairs (with which the synchronous speed is 1000 rpms).

In the following sections the data corresponding to these two connection possibilities are shown.

rpm: revolutions per second.

I: effective stator current in Amps.

P: Active power (in kW).

Q: Reactive power (in kVA).

S: Total power (in kVA).

Power factor (Cos φ).

Mechanical torque: in N*m.

The first table corresponds to the 4 magnetic pole connection and the second to 6.
