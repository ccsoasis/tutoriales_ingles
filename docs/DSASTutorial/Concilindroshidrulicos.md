# With electric motors

In this case, the blades are fixed to a crown with gears that have an associated motor. See the following figure.

![](<lib/Pitch electrico - Moog.jpg>)

These motors are governed by servo systems located inside the Hub, which respond to commands emitted by the Controller. Their actuation, in both directions, changes the corresponding blade angle value.

These servo systems and motors are powered by *uninterruptible power supplies*, which must guarantee enough energy to move the blades to the "feathered" position at any moment. *This is necessary to guarantee that, even if the wind turbine loses its electrical grid connection, it is possible to stop the blade rotation*.
