# Algorithms used

The algorithms used correspond to:

- The **blade angle** control. It affects the adaptation functions for the existing wind, start-up (cut-in), and disconnection (cut-out) procedures, as well as the safety stop procedures.

- The **brake hydraulic circuit pressure** control. It affects the pump and braking valve control.

- The **nacelle orientation** control. It controls the yaw motors.

- The [power cable unwinding](Yawingofthenacelle.md) control. It controls the yaw motors.

- The Grid value monitoring.

- The monitoring of events that affect operational safety.
