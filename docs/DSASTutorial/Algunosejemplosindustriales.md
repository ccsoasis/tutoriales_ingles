# Gearbox lubrication

The gearbox transmits all the mechanical power the wind turbine produces, and therefore all its elements (pinions, bearings, rolling surfaces, etc.) experience high stresses and mechanical loads; they must be adequately lubricated.

*The oil used in the lubrication avoids contact between the rolling and sliding surfaces in all the rotating elements*. Therefore, an important part of the lubrication system keeps the *oil free of contaminants*and suspended particles that could interpose and allow physical contact. Suspended particles can come from pinion, bearing, etc. breakage and wear.

These systems also incorporate heat exchangers to keep the oil temperature in adequate ranges.
