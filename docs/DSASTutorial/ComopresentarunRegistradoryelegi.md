# Panel 'Cutin + Cutout + Production'

The following figure shows a recorder with the panel called "Cutin + Cutout + Production".

![](<lib/RegistradorCutinCutoutProduccion.jpg>)

- 1.In the first 'channel' two signals are presented:

- 1.Signal generated in the Controller that serves as the blade **Pitch** *setpoint value*.

- 2.Signal coming from the hub simulator, with the real blade Pitch value.

- 2.In the second 'channel' a single signal is presented, corresponding to the **generated electrical power**.

- 3.In the third 'channel' three digital signals are presented:

- 1.The **Thyristors** activation signal.

- 2.The **'Brakes released'** signal.

- 3.The **Thyristor Bypass Contactor** activation signal.

- 4.In the fourth 'channel' a single signal with the **electric generator shaft rotation speed** (in rpms) is presented.

- 5.In the fifth 'channel' two signals are presented:

- 1.The **wind direction**.

- 2.The **nacelle orientation**.

- 6.In the sixth 'channel' two signals are presented:

- 1.The **wind speed**.

- 2.The **effective wind speed**.

This Panel is very useful to study the wind turbine evolution during the **grid connection** (*cutin*), **grid disconnection** (*cutout*), wind direction change, wind speed change operations.
