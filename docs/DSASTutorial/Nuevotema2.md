# Yaw mechanism brakes

The nacelle yawing mechanism incorporates a position fixing system whose mission is to avoid that orientation changing due to:

- The mechanical torque produced by the wind non-symmetry around the tower.

- The mechanical torque produced by the blade rotation speed changes, through the *gyroscopic effect*.

The torque created by this gyroscopic effect is perpendicular to the blade rotation axis, and can be very important if an abrupt blade rotation speed change occurs.
