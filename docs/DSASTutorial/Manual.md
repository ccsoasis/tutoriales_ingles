# Manual

The **Manual mode** is used when the Operator is present at the wind turbine and wants to control it manually.

The most significant operations in this mode are:

- The nacelle yawing.

- The blade pitch angle.

In addition they can know the value of the wind turbine internal signals:

- blade shaft and generator shaft revolutions

- temperature sensors in the various components

- real angle of each of the blades (offsets)

- etc.

From Manual mode one can switch to Automatic mode.
