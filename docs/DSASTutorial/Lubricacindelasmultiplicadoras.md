# Gearbox inspection

The repair or replacement of a gearbox is an important maintenance operation, which entails both direct costs (the repair or replacement itself), and indirect ones: crane and auxiliary material rental, logistical availability of spare parts, and lost profits due to the time the wind turbine is not in production.

Therefore, modernly, continuous gearbox monitoring equipment is being installed: this equipment continuously measures parameters such as the oil temperature, the amount of suspended particles in the oil, and, above all, the vibrations generated by the internal elements. This equipment is known as "Preventive Maintenance" or also as "***Condition Monitoring***" (***CMS*** or ***Condition Monitoring Systems***, in English).

The objective of this equipment is to provide criteria to decide to make gearbox inspections. These inspections have a small cost (basically the lost profit of putting the wind turbine out of production for a few hours), and are done using ocular inspection through equipment (*videoscopes*) based on optical fiber. The optical fiber, with separate channels for illumination and observation camera, is introduced through the gearbox casing orifices, and allows making observations, like the ones shown in the following video.
