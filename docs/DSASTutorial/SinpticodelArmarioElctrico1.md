# Interior view

![](<lib/Vista interna armario electrico espanol.png>)

Figura 1.

The interior view (see figure) shows the following aspects:

![](<lib/Armario electrico vista interna comentada esp.png>)

Figura 2.

The right panel contains the Main Switch, which is connected to the power grid cables (which enter from below) and connects to the busbars on that panel. The busbars on the left side of the central panel are connected via fuses to the busbars on the first panel. The difference in color between the busbars in Figures 1 and 2 is due to the position of the Main Switch.

The voltage of the busbars is typically 690 V.
