# How to present a Recorder and choose a predefined Panel in it.

The "**Recorders**" are synoptics where the temporal signal evolution is presented.

A "Recorder" can present several *predefined signal groups*, <u>but only one at a time</u>. These signal groups are called "**Predefined panels**", and have been defined thinking of the signal visualization particularized for certain observations.

In each Panel there are a number of "**channels**", where 1 or more signals *'congruent in terms of values'* are presented. Each signal has an *assigned color* for its identification.

As many Recorders as desired can be created. To do so go to the "Running Simulator ..." synoptic and press the "*Synoptics*" --> "*Recorders*" button.

Any Recorder can be closed through the "X" button in its upper right part.

In any Recorder the "Predefined panel" presented in it can be changed: however, this will make the current content of the Panel being used be lost. You can previously make a "**Copy**" with the corresponding button in its menu.

You can also change the "**sampling period**" of the presented signals using the first menu button on the right "**Seconds per pixel** xx": the possible values are *200ms, 500 ms, 1 second, 2 seconds, 5 seconds*. If you change this period value the current Panel content will be erased.
