# Electrical Scheme View

![](<lib/Armario electrico - esquema - espaol.png>)

The figure shows the electrical scheme of the cabinet power part. It can be seen that the grid connection to the Generator is made through the Main Switch, the fuses, the bars, the thyristors or their bypass contactors, the central panel bars, the power cables that go down from the nacelle, and the Generator connection contactors, either the "Small" ("Low speed") or the "Large" ("High speed").

In [Connection circuit detail](ConexinalaRedElctrica.md) details of the different power connection elements can be seen.
