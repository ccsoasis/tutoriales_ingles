# Nacelle Orientation and Wind synoptic (SynopticWindNacelle)

![](<lib/SynopticWindNacelle espaol.png>)

This synoptic allows:

- **Changing the wind speed and its direction**.

- Showing the **nacelle orientation**.

- Showing the number of turns on themselves of the [power cables](Mecanismodeorientacinyaw2.md).

- Showing the **wind vane signal**.

- Showing the **yaw motor state**. "Turn Left" and "Turn Right".

- Showing the **existing orientation error** (left panel, difference between the line coming out of the nacelle and the center of the wind arrow.)

- Showing (right panel) at relative scale, the **incident wind** (in blue), the **wind due to the blade rotation** (in black) and the **total wind** incident on the blade edge (in red.)

- The **blade angle** (**pitch**)

- Showing **graphically the wind speed** in relation to the nominal wind (blue arrow in the left panel).
