# Aerodynamic brakes in fixed-pitch blades

In old wind turbines (before the year 2000) and low power (<= 300 kW) the blades did not have a mechanism to change their pitch angle, and therefore the previously described mechanism could not be used.

In these models an *aerodynamic brake mechanism* is used, consisting in that the blade end can rotate along the blade axis, as shown in the following figure.
