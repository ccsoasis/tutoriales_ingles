# Cup anemometer

This type of anemometer is the most traditional. It contains an angular encoder that generates a number of pulses per revolution. The frequency of these pulses depends on the speed of rotation of the cups, which in turn depends on the wind speed. This device is omnidirectional, meaning that it does not depend on the orientation of the nacelle in relation to the wind.
