# Hub

The blade and Hub mass are assumed in the Power Train simulation.

The other mechanism existing in the Hub is the Pitch one. It is simulated assuming linear response characterized by a response slope to changes (Slew rate).

![](<lib/PitchModule-Slope.png>)

The associated parameter is *NacellePitch.BladeSlewRate*.
